Pick a candle size (1 min to 1 hour): Change, Range, ATR and Vol % are measured over the last N candles of that size, where N is the candle count you choose (fewer reacts faster), and Vol % is the typical move per candle. Score (0–100) = how volatile a coin is compared with the other high-volume coins. Vol × above 1 means volatility is expanding vs. its earlier norm.
